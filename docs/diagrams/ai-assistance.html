How a commit gets reviewed

Three views of the same platform: the parts and how they talk, the states a commit moves through, and who does what once the AI has left its findings.

01 · Architecture

Bitbucket in, findings out

One Go binary runs as the API or the worker. Postgres holds both the data and the River job queue, so storing a webhook and queueing its review happen in one transaction.

Focal

Review worker

  • Reviews one commit or pull request per job; the diff comes from Bitbucket, never from the webhook.
  • Also polls branches (POLL_REPOS), syncs people and roles daily, and runs reconcile every 5 min.
  • REVIEWER_MODE=mock in compose: no LLM is called until you switch it to claude_cli.

Entry points

API server

  • Checks the webhook signature, then stores the delivery and queues the job in one transaction.
  • Serves /api/v1 only when API_TOKENS is set; no tokens means no routes.
  • Every human action (fix, send back, dismiss, close, review on/off) goes to the append-only audit log.

Boundaries

What stays where

  • The dashboard renders on the server and never sees the Bitbucket token.
  • Browsers get HTML only; the personal token sits in an httpOnly cookie.

02 · State machine

A commit's review status

commits.review_status is the single source of truth. A commit only shows failed once nothing is going to retry it.

Focal

When a commit fails

  • Only the last attempt sets failed; earlier errors go back to pending and River retries.
  • Usage and rate limits snooze the job instead of spending an attempt.
  • A worker shutting down puts the commit back without counting it.

Skip reasons

Why a commit is skipped

  • Review is off for the repository, or it is a merge commit.
  • No reviewable files in the diff.
  • Imported by backfill: history, not reviewed.

Self-repair

Reconcile

  • Every 5 min: work stuck 10 min with no live job is queued again.
  • GET /api/v1/health says when the pipeline stops moving.

03 · Swimlane

Author fixes, senior closes

After the AI review, every finding needs a human verdict before the review can be closed. Roles come from the API token.

Focal

Closing a review

  • Allowed once no finding is open: each is fixed or dismissed.
  • Sending a finding back on a closed review reopens the review.
  • Review again starts a fresh review with every finding open.

Roles

Who can do what

  • author marks only their own findings fixed.
  • senior, lead judge and close other people's work.
  • admin can do every step, also on the author's behalf.

Trail

Recorded

  • Who, when and the note, under each finding.
  • Every action also goes to the audit log.
  • My work lists what is yours to fix or close.
